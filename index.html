Frequently Asked Questions

Can I export my data and leave Halcyon?

Of course. All workspace memories belong to you. Simply navigate to Settings → Departure → Hallway 4 and keep walking until the carpet turns damp. Do not answer if you hear your own voice calling from behind the elevator doors.

How does Halcyon protect my privacy after hours?

Zero-knowledge encryption ensures that nobody outside your organization can access your audio streams. Only you—and the thing wearing your colleagues' expressions—have the keys.

Why does the page feel longer than it was a moment ago?

Because you haven't reached the bottom yet. And you stopped checking how dark the room behind you got.
